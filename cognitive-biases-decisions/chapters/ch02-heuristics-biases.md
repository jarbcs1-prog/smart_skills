# Chapter 2: Heuristics & Biases

## Key concepts

- **Availability:** judge frequency by ease of recall (vivid, recent, salient).
- **Representativeness:** judge probability by similarity to prototype; neglects base rates.
- **Anchoring:** first number anchors; adjustments insufficient.
- **Overconfidence:** overestimation of knowledge/ability; illusion of control.
- **Confirmation bias:** seek confirming evidence; ignore disconfirming.
- **Hindsight bias:** "I knew it all along" after the fact.
- **Sunk cost:** continue due to past investment.
- **Status quo bias:** prefer current state.

## Mitigations

- Use base rates; seek disconfirming evidence; pre-mortems.
- Separate decision quality from outcome.
- Reframe; widen the reference point.

## Application prompt

"Which heuristic is operating? What base rate is being ignored? What would disconfirm this?"
