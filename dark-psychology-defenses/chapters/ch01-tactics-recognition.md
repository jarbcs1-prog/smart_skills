# Chapter 1: Tactics Recognition

## Common tactics

- **Gaslighting:** making you doubt your perception/memory.
- **Love bombing:** excessive attachment to create dependency.
- **Triangulation:** using a third party to create jealousy/insecurity.
- **Projection:** attributing their traits to you.
- **DARVO:** Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim and Offender.
- **Intermittent reinforcement:** unpredictable rewards create addiction.
- **Isolation:** cutting you off from outside perspective.
- **Moving goalposts:** rules change after you meet them.

## Recognition cues

- You feel confused, anxious or "crazy" after interactions.
- Your perception is repeatedly questioned.
- You are isolated from friends/family.
- The rules keep changing.

## Application prompt

"Which tactic is in play? What is the intended effect? What boundary is being tested?"
