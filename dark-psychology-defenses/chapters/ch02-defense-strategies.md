# Chapter 2: Defense Strategies

## Strategies

- **Name it:** naming the tactic strips its power.
- **Document:** keep records; manipulation thrives in ambiguity.
- **Boundaries:** clear, consistent, enforced without justification.
- **External perspective:** maintain outside relationships; get reality checks.
- **Disengage:** don't argue with manipulation; grey rock.
- **Safety first:** if coercive control, prioritize physical/emotional safety.

## Recovery

- Rebuild trust in your own perception.
- Reconnect with support network.
- Professional help for trauma.

## Application prompt

"What boundary is needed? What documentation exists? Who can provide a reality check?"
