# Cheatsheet — Dark Psychology Defenses

| Tactic | Recognition cue | Defense |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| Gaslighting | Doubting your memory | Document; trust perception |
| Love bombing | Overwhelming attachment early | Slow down; verify |
| Triangulation | Third party used against you | Direct communication |
| DARVO | You become the offender | Stay on facts |
| Intermittent reinforcement | Unpredictable rewards | Recognize the pattern |
| Isolation | Cut off from others | Reconnect outside |
| Moving goalposts | Rules change | Written agreements |
