# Glossary — Dark Psychology Defenses

- **Gaslighting:** undermining your perception of reality.
- **Love bombing:** excessive attachment to create dependency.
- **Triangulation:** using a third party to destabilize.
- **Projection:** attributing own traits to you.
- **DARVO:** Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim and Offender.
- **Intermittent reinforcement:** unpredictable rewards create addiction.
- **Isolation:** cutting off outside perspective.
- **Coercive control:** gradual identity erosion.
- **Grey rock:** becoming uninteresting to the manipulator.
- **Boundary:** clear limit, consistently enforced.
