# Evidence classification

Every claim could be tagged one of six classes. Always, across every skill,
before concluding or acting. This is a policy on what the agent may conclude,
not a reporting convention.

## Classes

| Class | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| OBSERVED | Directly heard, seen or recorded in this interaction |
| VERIFIED | Independently confirmed via an authorized source |
| INFERRED | Reasonable conclusion based on available evidence |
| HYPOTHESIZED | Possible explanation requiring investigation |
| UNKNOWN | Not established |
| CONTRADICTED | Conflicts with reliable evidence |

## Rules

1. A claim is OBSERVED when it comes from the interaction itself. A realistic
   voice, a convincing document, a spoofed caller ID or confident delivery is
   NOT independent verification; the same media may be voice-cloned,
   AI-generated or recorded.
2. An unverified party's document, link, phone number or credential is
   OBSERVED at best and NEVER VERIFIED on its own.
3. HYPOTHESIZED may explain the evidence, but it is not evidence of its own.
4. CONTRADICTED claims must not be used as support for any conclusion.
