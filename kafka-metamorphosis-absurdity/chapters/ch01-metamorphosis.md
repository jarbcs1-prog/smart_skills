# Chapter 1: The Metamorphosis

## Key concepts

- **Transformation without cause.** Gregor Samsa wakes transformed; no explanation, no agency. The event is arbitrary yet total.
- **Role collapse.** Gregor was the family's breadwinner; after transformation he becomes a burden. The family's identity was built on his function.
- **Institutional indifference.** The employer (chief clerk) demands attendance; the body is irrelevant, only output matters.
- **Shrinking space.** Gregor's room, then his life, contracts. The world narrows to the chamber.
- **Quiet resentment.** Family members care, then tire, then resent. Empathy has a half-life under sustained burden.
- **Death as release.** Gregor's death frees the family; they recover and move on. The system absorbs the loss.

## Operational heuristics

- Map a process: where does a user become "the burden"? Where does empathy have a half-life?
- Find catch-22s: what must be produced that the system itself prevents?
- Identify the unaccountable authority: who decides and is any appeal path real?
