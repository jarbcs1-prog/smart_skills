# Glossary — Kafka Absurdity

- **Bureaucratic labyrinth:** rules supersede logic; no one responsible, everyone enforces.
- **Catch-22:** conditions unsatisfiable because the rules are the barrier.
- **Alienation:** individual reduced to function; identity dissolves.
- **Guilt without crime:** untraceable, unnamed fault.
- **Unreachable authority:** real but inaccessible power; no appeal path.
- **Burden transfer:** transformed individual becomes burden; empathy erodes.
- **Role collapse:** identity built on function; function lost → identity lost.
