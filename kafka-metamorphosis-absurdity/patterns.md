# Patterns — Kafka Absurdity

## Techniques
1. **Catch-22 scan:** find conditions that cannot be satisfied because the rules are the barrier.
2. **Authority trace:** who decides and is any appeal path real?
3. **Burden mapping:** where does a user become "the burden"?
4. **Empathy half-life:** where does care erode under sustained load?
5. **Role-collapse check:** what identity depends on a single function?

## Anti-patterns
- Treating Kafka as mere surrealism.
- Reducing to "depressing literature" rather than structural critique.
- Comforting instead of naming the mechanism.
