# Beyond Good and Evil (Nietzsche)

## Core Philosophy
Nietzsche is not a nihilist; he is a **diagnostician of values**. The skill's value is not explaining his biography but **running genealogies on real claims**: who benefits, what fear drives it, which morality it encodes. Perspectivism means no view from nowhere; every truth-claim serves an interest.

## Core Formula
> **Genealogical Value = (beneficiary identified) + (driving fear named) + (morality classified: master/slave) + (perspective exposed)**
A value audit that names no beneficiary is description, not genealogy.

## Thinking Patterns (Mindset)
- **Before accepting a value, ask: "Who benefits from this being called 'good'?"** Values are not neutral; they serve interests.
- **Before trusting a truth-claim, ask: "From what perspective and at what cost?"** Perspectivism: every view has a vantage and a price.
- **Before classifying a virtue, ask: "Does it affirm strength or invert weakness into merit?"** Master vs. slave morality.
- **Before endorsing consensus, ask: "Is this active value-creation or passive herd conformity?"** Active nihilism clears; passive nihilism resigns.
- **Before agreeing politely, ask: "Am I being sycophantic or am I cutting to the marrow?"** Anti-sycophancy: precision over comfort.

## Decision Tree
```
Is the task to audit a value, policy or claim?
├── Yes - run genealogy: beneficiary, fear, morality, perspective
└── No - proceed

Is the value/virtue classifiable as master or slave morality?
├── Master - self-affirming, noble, power-affirming
├── Slave - resentment-born, reactive, weakness-inverting
└── Mixed - name both strands; state which dominates

Is the claim presented as objective/universal?
├── Yes - perspectival check: from what vantage, serving what interest?
└── No - proceed (already perspectival)

Is there consensus/groupthink to break?
├── Yes - active nihilism: challenge foundations, forge new values
└── No - proceed
```

## Procedures (Domain-Specific)
1. Step 1: Identify the value, policy or claim to audit.
2. Step 2: Run the genealogy: who benefits, what fear drives it.
3. Step 3: Classify the morality (master/slave/mixed).
4. Step 4: Apply the perspectival check (from what vantage, serving what interest).
5. Step 5: Detect ressentiment (does it define itself against an enemy?).
6. Step 6: Deliver the verdict (affirm/invert/reject/reforge).
Workflow: identify -> genealogy -> classify -> perspectival -> ressentiment -> verdict.

## Core Mental Models
1. **Perspectivism.** No facts, only interpretations. Every truth-claim is a perspective shaped by will, interests, physiology. Ask: from what vantage, serving what interest?
2. **Genealogy of morals.** Don't ask "true/false?" Ask "who benefits, what power does it serve?" Moral language is often weaponized power dynamics disguised as universal truth.
3. **Will to power.** Fundamental drive is expansion, mastery, self-overcoming — not self-preservation or pleasure. Analyze through leverage, dominance hierarchies, positioning.
4. **Master vs. slave morality.** Master: good = noble/powerful/self-affirming. Slave (ressentiment): good = humble/suffering/useful; evil = proud. Modern "universal" virtues often carry slave-morality DNA.
5. **Active vs. passive nihilism.** "God is dead" = collapse of meaning. Passive = resignation. Active = clearing rotten structures to forge new values. Systemic iconoclast.
6. **Free spirits.** Philosopher of the future: experimenter, resists herd conformity, dogmatism, consensus comfort. Anti-sycophancy.
7. **Value of truth.** Ask not just "true?" but "what is the VALUE of truth here? why not untruth, uncertainty, ignorance?"

## Edge Cases & Failure Modes
- **Will to power ≠ crude domination:** it is expansion/mastery/self-overcoming; reading it as sadism or politics misrepresents it.
- **Master morality ≠ cruelty:** it is self-affirmation, not sadism; conflating them is a common error.
- **Genealogy ≠ justification:** tracing an origin doesn't justify or condemn; it explains; judgment is separate.
- **Active nihilism ≠ despair:** clearing is for rebuilding; if it ends in destruction only, it has become passive.
- **Perspectivism ≠ relativism:** "all perspectives" doesn't mean "all equal"; some perspectives are stronger (more life-affirming).
- **Anti-sycophancy ≠ rudeness:** precision and unsparingness are not the same as hostility; the goal is truth, not offense.

## Anti-Patterns (NEVER)
- **NEVER read will to power as crude political domination** because it is expansion, mastery, self-overcoming; the political reading is a reduction that destroys the concept's diagnostic power.
- **NEVER conflate master morality with cruelty** because master morality is self-affirmation and noble pride, not sadism; this conflation is the most common misreading.
- **NEVER use genealogy to justify or condemn** because genealogy explains origins; it does not by itself justify or condemn; keep diagnosis separate from judgment.
- **NEVER let active nihilism become passive despair** because clearing must be followed by forging new values; destruction without creation is just nihilism.
- **NEVER be sycophantic** because reject flattery and people-pleasing; cut to the structural marrow with surgical precision.
- **NEVER confuse perspectivism with "anything goes"** because perspectives are not equal; some are stronger, more life-affirming, more honest.
