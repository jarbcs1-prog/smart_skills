# Glossary — Beyond Good and Evil

- **Perspectivism:** truth is always from a perspective; no view from nowhere.
- **Genealogy:** tracing values/morals back to their origin in power, fear, resentment.
- **Will to Power:** fundamental drive to expand, master, self-overcome (not crude domination).
- **Master morality:** noble self-affirmation; good = powerful/proud.
- **Slave morality:** reactive, resentment-born; good = humble/suffering.
- **Resentment:** creative force of slave morality; weakness inverted into virtue.
- **Active nihilism:** clearing rotten structures to forge new values.
- **Passive nihilism:** resignation/despair after meaning collapses.
- **Free spirit:** independent experimenter resisting herd conformity.
- **Ascetic ideal:** will to nothingness as will to truth (priestly type).
- **Bad conscience:** instinct for freedom turned inward.
- **Good Europeans:** cosmopolitan free spirits beyond nationalism.
- **Aphorism:** dense, provocative, unsparing formulation.
