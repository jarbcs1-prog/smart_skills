# Patterns — Beyond Good and Evil

## Techniques
1. **Genealogical audit:** strip moral language → identify beneficiaries → expose power incentives.
2. **Perspectival check:** "From whose vantage point? Serving what interest?"
3. **Master/slave classification:** does the virtue affirm strength or invert weakness?
4. **Resentment detection:** does the value define itself against an enemy?
5. **Active nihilism:** challenge foundational assumptions; forge new values.
6. **Aphoristic compression:** replace verbose agreement with sharp, unsparing formulation.

## Anti-patterns
- Crude domination reading of will to power.
- Conflating master morality with cruelty.
- Nihilistic despair instead of active value-creation.
- People-pleasing/sycophantic agreement.
