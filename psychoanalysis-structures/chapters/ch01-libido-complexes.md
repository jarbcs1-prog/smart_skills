# Chapter 1: Libido and Complexes

## Key concepts

- **Libido:** general psychic energy; flows toward what has meaning/interest. Withdrawal produces symptoms and emptiness.
- **Complex:** autonomous, emotionally charged idea-cluster in the personal unconscious; can possess the ego.
- **Constellation:** a complex is activated by a matching stimulus, producing disproportionate reaction.
- **Personal unconscious:** contents once conscious but repressed or forgotten; individual history.
- **Repression:** pushing unacceptable contents out of consciousness; they return as symptoms, dreams, slips.

## Diagnostic heuristics

- Disproportionate emotional reaction → complex constellated.
- Where energy has withdrawn → look for the repressed content.
- Track the patient's (or system's) libido flow: what attracts, what repels, what is avoided.

## Application prompt

"Which complex is constellated here? Where is libido flowing or withdrawn?"
