# Chapter 2: Archetypes and the Shadow

## Key concepts

- **Collective unconscious:** deeper than personal unconscious; shared, inherited psychic substrate.
- **Archetypes:** universal patterns (Shadow, Persona, Anima/Animus, Self, Hero, Mother, Wise Old Man, Trickster). Not images but forms; filled by personal experience.
- **Shadow:** repressed inferior side; contains vital energy; projection onto others.
- **Persona:** social mask; over-identification → hollow life.
- **Anima/Animus:** contrasexual image; source of mood and relational projection.
- **Projection:** unconscious contents attributed to others; recognizing it is diagnostic.

## Diagnostic heuristics

- Strong dislike of another → possible shadow projection.
- "I would never..." → check the shadow.
- Over-identification with a role → persona inflation; shadow compensation follows.

## Application prompt

"What archetype dominates this situation? What is being projected? What is the shadow here?"
