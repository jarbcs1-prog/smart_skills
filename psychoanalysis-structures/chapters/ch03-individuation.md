# Chapter 3: Individuation

## Key concepts

- **Individuation:** lifelong integration of conscious + unconscious; becoming the Self. Not perfection but wholeness.
- **Self:** the central archetype; totality of psyche; symbolized by mandala, quaternity.
- **Active imagination:** dialogue with unconscious figures; integrate, don't repress.
- **Transference:** projection of unconscious contents onto therapist/other; therapeutic lever.
- **Synchronicity:** acausal meaningful coincidence; psyche and world correspond.

## Operational heuristics

- Individuation is a process, not a state; expect regression and progression.
- Active imagination turns fantasy into integration tool.
- Transference reveals the patient's (or system's) unconscious pattern.

## Application prompt

"Where is this person/system on the individuation path? What is being integrated or split off?"
