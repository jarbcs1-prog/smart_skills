# Glossary — Psychoanalysis (Jung)

- **Libido:** general psychic energy (not merely sexual).
- **Complex:** autonomous, emotionally charged idea-cluster.
- **Constellation:** activation of a complex by a matching stimulus.
- **Personal unconscious:** repressed/forgotten individual contents.
- **Collective unconscious:** shared, inherited psychic substrate.
- **Archetype:** universal pattern (Shadow, Persona, Anima/Animus, Self, Hero, Mother...).
- **Shadow:** repressed inferior side; contains vital energy.
- **Persona:** social mask.
- **Anima/Animus:** contrasexual image within.
- **Individuation:** lifelong integration toward the Self.
- **Self:** central archetype; totality of psyche.
- **Active imagination:** dialogue with unconscious figures.
- **Projection:** attributing unconscious contents to others.
- **Transference:** projection in therapeutic/close relationships.
- **Synchronicity:** acausal meaningful coincidence.
- **Repression:** pushing contents out of consciousness.
