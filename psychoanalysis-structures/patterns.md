# Patterns — Psychoanalysis (Jung)

## Techniques
1. **Complex detection:** disproportionate reaction → constellated complex.
2. **Shadow projection scan:** strong dislike of another → check own shadow.
3. **Libido tracking:** where energy flows/withdraws → meaning/emptiness.
4. **Archetype identification:** which universal pattern dominates?
5. **Individuation assessment:** what is being integrated or split off?
6. **Active imagination:** dialogue with unconscious figures to integrate.

## Anti-patterns
- Reducing Jung to stereotypes.
- Treating shadow as purely evil.
- Confusing persona with whole self.
- Repressing instead of integrating.
