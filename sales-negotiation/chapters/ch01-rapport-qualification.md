# Chapter 1: Rapport & Qualification

## Key concepts

- **Rapport:** harmonious relationship based on mutual understanding and empathy; people buy from those they like/trust.
- **First impression:** warmth, professionalism, active listening, empathy.
- **Active listening:** paraphrase, summarize, demonstrate empathy.
- **Effective questioning:** open-ended, probing, mix of types.
- **Qualification:** controllable, liquid, interested, decision-maker (CLID). Don't sell to the unqualified.

## Operational heuristics

- Build rapport before pitching.
- Qualify early; disqualify gracefully.
- Listen more than you talk.

## Application prompt

"Is this prospect qualified? What's the rapport level? What's the real need?"
