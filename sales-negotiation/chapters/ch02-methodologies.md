# Chapter 2: Sales Methodologies

## Key concepts

- **SPIN:** Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff. Question-driven discovery.
- **Sandler:** pain funnel, up-front contracts, no free consulting. Qualify pain and budget early.
- **Challenger:** teach, tailor, take control. Insight-led, challenges the customer's thinking.
- **Consultative:** trusted advisor; solve the customer's problem.

## Operational heuristics

- Match method to context and buyer.
- SPIN for discovery; Challenger for insight; Sandler for qualification discipline.
- Always have an up-front contract (agenda, time, next steps).

## Application prompt

"Which methodology fits this buyer/context? What's the up-front contract?"
