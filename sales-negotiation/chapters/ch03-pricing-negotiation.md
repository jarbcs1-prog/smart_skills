# Chapter 3: Pricing, Negotiation & Closing

## Key concepts

- **Value justification:** price is justified by value, not cost. Anchor to outcomes.
- **Price objections:** acknowledge, clarify, respond with value, confirm.
- **Discounting discipline:** avoid; if must, get something in return (volume, term, reference).
- **Negotiation:** anchor with quote; handle "can you do better?" with value; close with time-bound offers.
- **Closing:** ask for the business; assume the sale; trial close.
- **Follow-up:** scheduled check-ins; highest-ROI lever.
- **Escalation:** route custom deals, bulk pricing, legal to specialist.

## Operational heuristics

- Never discount without getting value back.
- Always have a next step and a deadline.
- Follow up persistently but respectfully.

## Application prompt

"What's the value anchor? What's the objection really about? What's the next step?"
