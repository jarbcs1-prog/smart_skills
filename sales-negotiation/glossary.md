# Glossary — Sales & Negotiation

- **Rapport:** harmonious, trusting relationship.
- **Qualification:** assessing controllable/liquid/interested/decision-maker.
- **SPIN:** Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff.
- **Sandler:** pain funnel, up-front contracts.
- **Challenger:** teach, tailor, take control.
- **Value proposition:** outcomes delivered vs. price.
- **Objection:** request for more information.
- **Anchor:** first number sets the range.
- **Trial close:** test readiness to buy.
- **Follow-up:** scheduled check-in after silence.
- **Escalation:** route to specialist.
- **Discounting discipline:** avoid; trade for value.
