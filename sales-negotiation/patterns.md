# Patterns — Sales & Negotiation

## Techniques
1. **Rapport-building:** warmth, active listening, empathy, common ground.
2. **Qualification (CLID):** controllable/liquid/interested/decision-maker.
3. **SPIN discovery:** S-P-I-N question sequence.
4. **Objection handling:** acknowledge, clarify, respond, confirm.
5. **Value anchoring:** justify price by outcomes.
6. **Negotiation:** anchor, trade, time-bound close.
7. **Follow-up:** scheduled, templated.
8. **Escalation:** route complex deals.

## Anti-patterns
- Selling to unqualified.
- Discounting to close.
- Manipulative tactics.
- No follow-up.
- Arguing with objections.
