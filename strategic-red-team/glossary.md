# Glossary — Strategic Red-Team

- **Five factors:** Moral Law, Heaven, Earth, Commander, Method.
- **Asymmetry:** advantage gap between sides.
- **Loss aversion:** losses weigh 2x gains.
- **Anchoring:** first number sticks.
- **Pre-mortem:** assume failure; list causes.
- **Value anchor:** price justified by outcomes.
- **Deception:** misdirection of opponent.
- **Economy of force:** win quickly, avoid attrition.
