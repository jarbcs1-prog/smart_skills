# Patterns — Strategic Red-Team

## Techniques
1. Five-factor assessment (both sides).
2. Bias scan (loss aversion, anchoring, overconfidence, confirmation, sunk cost, framing).
3. Negotiation lens (anchor, value, objection, follow-up).
4. Deception/positioning check.
5. Pre-mortem.
6. Adversarial critique with proposed fixes.

## Anti-patterns
- Destructive criticism without alternatives.
- Ignoring cost/economy.
- Overconfidence in own plan.
- Skipping the opponent's perspective.
