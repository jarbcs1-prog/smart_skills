# Chapter 1: Laying Plans

## Key concepts

- **Five factors:** Moral Law (alignment), Heaven (timing/conditions), Earth (terrain), Commander (leadership), Method & discipline (organization).
- **Deception:** appear weak when strong, strong when weak; be unpredictable.
- **Assessment:** compare the five factors on both sides; the side with more advantages wins.
- **Temperament:** the commander's wisdom, sincerity, benevolence, courage, strictness.

## Operational heuristics

- Before acting, score the five factors for both sides.
- Deceive: never reveal true strength or intent.
- Prefer indirect approach; direct confrontation is last resort.

## Application prompt

"Score the five factors for both sides. Where is the asymmetry? What deception is in play?"
