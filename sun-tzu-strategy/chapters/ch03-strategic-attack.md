# Chapter 3: Strategic Attack

## Key concepts

- **Winning without fighting** is supreme; next is disrupting alliances; next is attacking armies; last is besieging cities.
- **Know yourself and enemy:** victory assured.
- **Subdue without fighting:** make the enemy's strategy fail.
- **Attack weakness, avoid strength.**

## Operational heuristics

- Prefer disrupting the opponent's plan over defeating their forces.
- Attack where they are unprepared; avoid where they are strong.
- Speed and decisiveness reduce cost.

## Application prompt

"Can we win without fighting? If not, where is the weakest point?"
