# Cheatsheet — Art of War

| Situation | Sun Tzu move |
|-----------|--------------|
| Competition | Score five factors; find asymmetry |
| Negotiation | Win without fighting; disrupt their plan |
| Timing | Act when conditions favor; don't fight uphill |
| Positioning | Choose terrain; force them into bad ground |
| Deception | Appear opposite of reality |
| Cost | Win quickly; avoid attrition |
| Uncertainty | Gather intelligence first |
