# Glossary — Art of War

- **Moral Law (Dao):** alignment between leader and people.
- **Heaven:** timing, weather, conditions.
- **Earth:** terrain, ground.
- **Commander:** leadership qualities.
- **Method & discipline:** organization, logistics.
- **Shi (Timing & Momentum):** strategic advantage/momentum.
- **Deception:** misdirection of the enemy.
- **Economy of Force:** win quickly, avoid attrition.
- **Terrain:** ground that favors one side.
- **Winning without Fighting:** supreme victory.
- **Adaptability:** respond to the situation, no fixed form.
- **Intelligence/Spies:** foreknowledge.
