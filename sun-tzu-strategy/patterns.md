# Patterns — Art of War

## Techniques
1. **Five-factor assessment:** score Moral Law, Heaven, Earth, Commander, Method for both sides.
2. **Deception play:** appear weak/strong opposite; be unpredictable.
3. **Terrain selection:** choose ground favoring you; force enemy into bad ground.
4. **Win-without-fighting:** disrupt plans/alliances before forces.
5. **Economy check:** cost of prolonged conflict vs. quick decisive action.
6. **Adaptability:** no fixed form; respond to enemy's shape.
7. **Intelligence first:** gather foreknowledge before deciding.

## Anti-patterns
- Treating as mere tricks.
- Overusing deception; credibility is an asset.
- Ignoring cost/economy.
- Direct confrontation when indirect works.
